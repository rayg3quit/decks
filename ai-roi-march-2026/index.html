Beyond Token Math

Strategic value to Rev.io

Dog-fooding AI operations. Rev.io sells AI capabilities to MSPs. Our CTO, CEO, and COO running AI staff members is not just efficiency — it's product intelligence. Every API gap Simon hits, every workflow that works or fails, directly informs what we build for customers.

Organizational AI literacy. 32 keys = 20+ humans using AI assistants daily. This is not a pilot. This is institutional capability building. When competitors are still debating AI policy, Rev.io's leadership team has 2 months of operational experience.

Compounding returns. Every tool Simon builds, every workflow Revay automates, every integration Romer identifies — it persists. Month 2 is cheaper and more capable than Month 1. The AI fleet gets better while humans plateau.
